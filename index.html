Record this device's route while you hunt. The track is saved on this device only; live tracking other hunters requires a secure hosted service and their opt-in. Mobile browsers may pause GPS when you switch apps or lock the phone. FieldTrack checkpoints the route and asks you to resume; it does not draw a straight line across the gap.

Privacy & limits

Pins are stored locally in this browser and can be exported as GeoJSON. Weather/elevation requests need an internet connection. Map tiles and public APIs have their own availability and usage limits. This prototype is not an official land-access or navigation authority.
